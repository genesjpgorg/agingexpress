# Source attribution and terms

- **HCATA:** Bartz J., Ma X., Zhang L. et al. *Human Cell Aging Transcriptome Atlas (HCATA): a single-cell atlas of age-associated transcriptomic alterations across human tissues.* Communications Biology 8, 1450 (2025). DOI: [10.1038/s42003-025-08845-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-025-08845-8). The paper's stated license is [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/). The reference records here come from the public [HCATA API/portal](http://hcata-xiaodonglab.org:3304/). Article text and figures are not reproduced. The raw JSON responses are archived unchanged, with gzip compression; the TSVs convert identifiers, decode probabilities, select the muscle cohort and add explicitly named derived contrasts.
- **Muscle cohort:** *Single nuclei profiling identifies cell specific markers of skeletal muscle aging, frailty, and senescence.* Aging 14(23), 9393–9422 (2022). PMID [36516485](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36516485/), DOI [10.18632/aging.204435](https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.204435), GEO [GSE167186](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE167186). Public GEO series/sample metadata are archived unchanged with gzip compression. Donor labels are the published pseudonymous study IDs.
- **Gene selection:** The earlier frozen panel used HAGR age-up/down lists and GSE5086/GSE40645 gene mappings, with GSE5086 expression matching for background genes. [`panel_provenance.json`](data/hcata_muscle_v1/panel_provenance.json) records the original selection, seed, control IDs and source hashes. HAGR selection labels are not HCATA outcomes. HGNC mappings were taken from the cited Indra mirror snapshot.
